## Aseptic Products Assessment

### Critical Finding: NO ASEPTIC PRODUCTS EVIDENT

**Mariposa Andina:**
- ✗ No aseptic products in export data
- ✗ Only IQF frozen products
- ⚠️ Prospectus doesn't mention aseptic capability

**Chavin de Huantar:**
- ✗ No aseptic products in this export dataset
- ✗ Only IQF frozen products observed
- ⚠️ Prospectus mentions frozen and dried, not aseptic

**Conclusion:**
If your business model requires aseptic products (shelf-stable, no refrigeration), **NEITHER company appears suitable based on actual export data**. Both focus on IQF frozen products requiring cold chain.

**Recommendation:**
- Confirm aseptic capability directly with both companies
- If aseptic is required, may need to source from different Peruvian suppliers
- If frozen IQF is acceptable, both companies are viable

## Final Recommendations

### Choose Chavin de Huantar IF:
1. ✓ You need **volume** (100+ tons/month)
2. ✓ **Mango and/or avocado** IQF are your core products
3. ✓ You value **proven European track record**
4. ✓ You're entering **multiple EU markets**
5. ✓ You need **reliable, consistent supply**
6. ✓ You have **B2B/food service** focus
7. ✓ **Frozen IQF** is acceptable (not aseptic)

**Ideal Buyer Profile:** Established frozen fruit importer/distributor with multi-country EU network, seeking volume supplier for mango/avocado

### Choose Mariposa Andina IF:
1. ✓ You need **specialty/exotic** fruits
2. ✓ **Lúcuma, chirimoya** are valuable for differentiation
3. ✓ You operate in **premium/specialty** segment
4. ✓ You can work with **smaller volumes** (50-100 tons/month)
5. ✓ You can **develop the supplier** (limited EU experience)
6. ✓ You value **product diversity**
7. ✓ **Frozen IQF** is acceptable (not aseptic)

**Ideal Buyer Profile:** Specialty food importer, premium retail buyer, or innovative food service operator seeking unique products

### Don't Choose Either IF:
1. ✗ You specifically need **aseptic** (shelf-stable) products
2. ✗ You need **fresh** fruits (this data shows frozen only)
3. ✗ You need **dried/dehydrated** products as primary focus
4. ✗ You need **vegetables** (neither is active in vegetables)

## Conclusion

**Chavin de Huantar** is the **clear choice** for a serious European market entry requiring:
- Proven capability (9 EU countries, $13.9M exports)
- Scale (15,850 tons/year)
- Reliability (826 shipments, consistent operations)
- Core products (mango, avocado) with EU demand

**Mariposa Andina** offers **unique value** for specialty/premium positioning:
- Differentiated products (lúcuma, chirimoya)
- Premium pricing justifiable
- Opportunity for first-mover advantage
- Requires more development/risk tolerance

**Critical Gap:** Neither company shows **aseptic product** capability in export data. If aseptic is essential to your business model, further investigation required or alternative suppliers needed.

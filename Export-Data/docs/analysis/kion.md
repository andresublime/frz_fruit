# Ginger (Kion/Jengibre) Export Analysis - Peru

**Report Date:** December 3, 2025
**Data Period:** January 2024 - October 2025
**Source:** Peru Frozen Fruit Exports Database

---

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes ginger exports from Peru, focusing on commercial shipments, producers, formats, and pricing. Ginger exports from Peru show emerging commercial viability with **11 shipments** from **3 producers** totaling **25.84 metric tons**.

**Key Findings:**
- Commercial prices range from **$2,271 to $3,572 per MT** for organic certified products
- All major commercial shipments are **organic certified**
- Primary format is **IQF (Individually Quick Frozen)** chunks/pieces
- Main markets: **United States, Netherlands, and Poland**
- Value-added juice mixes command premium pricing ($3,572/MT vs $2,271/MT for pure IQF)

## Glossary

**Kion** - Peruvian Spanish term for ginger (Zingiber officinale)

**Jengibre** - Standard Spanish term for ginger

**IQF** - Individually Quick Frozen; freezing method that prevents pieces from clumping

**Trozos** - Chunks/pieces (Spanish)

**Semipelado** - Semi-peeled (Spanish)

**Cubos** - Cubes (Spanish)

**FOB** - Free On Board; price includes delivery to ship but not shipping costs

**MT** - Metric Ton (1,000 kg)

**MARÍTIMO** - Maritime/sea shipping

**AÉREO** - Air freight

**MUESTRA SIN VALOR COMERCIAL** - Sample without commercial value

**Cúrcuma/Turmeric** - Related root often combined with ginger (Curcuma longa)

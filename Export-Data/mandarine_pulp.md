# Mandarine Pulp Export Analysis - Peru

**Report Date:** December 3, 2025
**Data Period:** January 2024 - September 2025
**Source:** Peru Frozen Fruit Exports Database

---

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes mandarine pulp exports from Peru. **Mandarine pulp exports are minimal** - only **2 sample shipments** totaling **0.003 MT** were found, both sent via air freight to the United Kingdom.

However, the broader **frozen mandarine market shows commercial viability** with **27 total shipments** from **11 producers** totaling **94.27 MT**, primarily in frozen chunks/segments format rather than pulp.

**Key Findings - Mandarine Pulp:**
- **Only 2 exports found** - both small air freight samples
- **Single producer**: PROCESADORA LARAN SAC
- **Single market**: United Kingdom
- **No commercial-scale pulp shipments identified**
- Sample pricing: $5,000-$10,000/MT (unreliable - air freight samples)

**Key Findings - Frozen Mandarine (All Formats):**
- Commercial prices: **$1,256 - $1,829/MT** for frozen segments/chunks
- **Chile is dominant market** (71 MT, 75% of volume)
- Main format: **Frozen chunks/segments** in 10 kg boxes
- Leading exporter: **MEBOL SAC** (59 MT commercial shipments to Chile)

- All via expensive air freight
- Single producer testing single market (UK)
- No evidence of buyer interest or follow-up orders

⚠️ **Limited producer interest**
- Only 1 of 11 mandarine exporters produces pulp
- Main exporters (MEBOL, DOMINUS) focus on frozen chunks/whole
- Suggests pulp format not commercially viable for mandarine

💡 **Market development stage**
- Recent samples (Nov-Dec 2024) indicate initial testing
- UK market being explored
- No commercial traction observed

### Frozen Mandarine (General)

✅ **Established commercial market**
- 72 MT commercial shipments demonstrate viability
- Consistent pricing ($1,256-$1,829/MT)
- Repeat orders to Chile market

✅ **Dominant producer identified**
- MEBOL SAC controls 63% of commercial market
- Consistent quality and pricing
- Established Chile trade relationship

✅ **Chile market opportunity**
- 71 MT to Chile (76% of volume)
- Year-round demand
- Proximity advantage for Peru

⚠️ **Limited format diversity**
- 93% of commercial volume is frozen chunks/whole
- Pulp format not gaining traction

⚠️ **Heavy reliance on samples**
- 21 of 27 shipments (78%) are samples
- Only 6 commercial shipments total
- Suggests many producers still testing markets

### Opportunities

💡 **Chile market expansion**
- Proven demand for 70+ MT/year
- Room for additional suppliers beyond MEBOL
- Pricing stable and profitable

💡 **Canada market development**
- Willing to pay premium ($1,800/MT vs $1,550/MT Chile)

💡 **European markets (France, UK)**
- Large sample to France (22 MT) suggests serious buyer interest
- UK testing pulp format - potential niche opportunity
- Premium pricing potential for specialty/organic products

💡 **Product format innovation**
- Pulp not working, but other value-added options exist
- Juice blends (like ginger-turmeric examples)
- Organic/specialty certifications
- Retail-ready packaging

### Challenges

⚠️ **Pulp format rejection**
- Market clearly prefers frozen chunks/whole over pulp
- Only 1 producer attempting pulp, with no success
- Processing costs may exceed market willingness to pay

⚠️ **Market concentration risk**
- 76% of volume to single market (Chile)
- MEBOL dominates with 63% market share
- Limited geographic diversification

⚠️ **Sample proliferation**
- Too many samples, too few conversions to commercial
- 11 producers but only 3 with commercial shipments
- Suggests market barriers or quality issues

⚠️ **Competition**
- Mandarine available from many origins
- Peru not traditional mandarine producer
- Price competition from China, Spain, Morocco

⚠️ **No organic certification observed**
- Zero organic-certified shipments found
- Missing premium pricing opportunity
- Specialty markets (UK, EU) prefer organic

## Conclusion

### Mandarine Pulp: Not Commercially Viable

Based on comprehensive database analysis, **mandarine pulp exports from Peru show no commercial viability**:
- Only 3 kg exported across 2 sample shipments
- Single producer, single market (UK)
- No commercial orders observed
- Air freight sample pricing unreliable ($5,000-$10,000/MT)

**Recommendation:** Producers should **avoid investing in mandarine pulp production** unless specific buyer commitments are secured. The market clearly prefers frozen chunks/segments format.

### Frozen Mandarine: Established Commercial Opportunity

In contrast, **frozen mandarine (non-pulp) shows strong commercial viability**:
- 72 MT commercial shipments demonstrate market demand
- Stable pricing: $1,256-$1,829/MT (avg ~$1,550/MT)
- Established Chile market (71 MT, 76% of volume)
- Growing trend: +63% volume increase 2024→2025
- Market leader identified: MEBOL SAC (45 MT commercial)

**Recommendation:** Producers should focus on **frozen chunks/segments in 10 kg boxes** targeting **Chile market** (primary) and **Canada market** (premium pricing, less competition).

### Strategic Direction

For mandarine exports from Peru:
1. ✅ **Prioritize:** Frozen chunks/segments to Chile and Canada
2. 💡 **Explore:** Value-added blends, organic certification for European markets
3. ⚠️ **Monitor:** France market (22 MT sample suggests opportunity)
4. ❌ **Avoid:** Pulp format unless specific buyer commitment secured

## Glossary

**Mandarina** - Spanish for mandarine/tangerine (Citrus reticulata)

**Pulpa** - Pulp; processed fruit flesh/juice

**Gajos** - Segments; individual sections of citrus fruit (Spanish)

**Chunks** - Pieces/chunks; common frozen fruit format

**C/piel** - Con piel; with peel/skin (Spanish)

**Semipelado** - Semi-peeled (Spanish)

**Muestra sin valor comercial** - Sample without commercial value

**FOB** - Free On Board; price includes delivery to ship

**MT** - Metric Ton (1,000 kg)

**MARÍTIMO** - Maritime/sea shipping (Spanish)

**AÉREO** - Air freight (Spanish)

## Executive Summary

FRUTOS TONGORRAPE is a **dual-capability Peruvian fruit processor** operating distinct **aseptic** (65.4% of volume) and **frozen/IQF** (34.6% of volume) business lines. The company exported **5,829.34 MT** worth **$13.37 million USD** across **395 shipments** to **19 countries** from January 2024 to October 2025.

**Primary Business:** ASEPTIC processor specializing in **passion fruit juice** (67% of aseptic volume)
**Secondary Business:** FROZEN/IQF mango and avocado products (20x20 chunks, IQF, cubes)

**Key Strengths:**
- Dominant aseptic passion fruit juice exporter to Europe
- Dual production capabilities (aseptic + frozen) = diversified revenue
- Higher margins on aseptic ($1,887/MT) vs frozen ($1,539/MT)
- Strong European distribution network (50% of volume)

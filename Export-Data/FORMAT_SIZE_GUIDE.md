## Size Code Interpretation

### Actual Piece Sizes (mm x mm)
- **20x20**: 20mm x 20mm square pieces
- **25x25**: 25mm x 25mm square pieces  
- **15x15**: 15mm x 15mm square pieces
- **10x10**: 10mm x 10mm small cubes
- **6x48**, **8x48**, **12x48**: Strawberry cuts (width x length)

### Package Format Codes
- **1x10**: 1kg bags, 10 bags per case
- **1x30**: 1kg bags, 30 bags per case
- **12x500**: 12 packages of 500g each
- **8x0**: 8kg packages
- **10x1**: 10kg per package, 1 per case

### Special Codes
- **20x500**: Could be 20 packages of 500g OR 20mm pieces in 500g packs
- **10x250**: 10 packages of 250g
- **N/A / No size**: Unspecified or mixed sizes

## Quality Standards

### Size Tolerance
Most size specifications appear to be ±2mm based on industry standards:
- 20x20mm actually means 18-22mm x 18-22mm
- 15x15mm means 13-17mm x 13-17mm

### Format Quality Hierarchy (by price):
1. **Halves/Whole** - Minimal processing, highest quality needed
2. **IQF** - Individual pieces, premium freezing method
3. **Cubes** - Precision cutting, uniform appearance
4. **Chunks** - Standard commercial grade
5. **Slices** - Variable quality
6. **Pulp** - By-product or over-ripe fruit acceptable
7. **Puree** - Lowest grade, appearance doesn't matter

## Recommendations for Buyers

### For **Premium Markets** (restaurants, retail):
- Request: **IQF** or **Halves**
- Sizes: **15x15mm** or **20x20mm cubes/chunks**
- Expect: $3,000 - $6,000/MT

### For **Food Service** (bulk users):
- Request: **Chunks 20x20mm** or **Cubes**
- Expect: $2,000 - $3,000/MT

### For **Processing** (smoothies, baby food):
- Request: **Puree** or **Pulp**
- Expect: $1,200 - $2,500/MT

### For **Manufacturing** (mixed products):
- Request: **Chunks (no size spec)** - Most economical
- Expect: $1,900 - $2,300/MT

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **20x20mm is the industry standard** for chunks across all fruits
2. **IQF and Halves command 2-3x premium** over basic formats
3. **Package codes (1x10, 1x30) are common** - don't confuse with piece sizes
4. **Avocado has highest format diversity** and premium pricing
5. **Berries (blueberry, strawberry) prefer Whole or IQF** - minimal cutting
6. **Size specification matters more for higher-value products**

## Company Overview
- **Legal Name:** Agricola y Ganadera Chavin de Huantar SA
- **Brand:** AG Chavin / Chavin
- **Founded:** 1999
- **Years in Operation:** 25+ years
- **Main Plant Locations:** 
  - Casma Valley, Ancash (380km north of Lima) - 3 specialized agro-food production plants
  - Lima, Peru
- **Industry Position:** Largest agro-food industry in northern Peru
- **Company Philosophy:** Named after pre-Inca Chavin civilization, aspires to its greatness and timelessness

## Business Lines
1. **Frozen Products** (85% of sales)
2. **Fresh Products** (palta/avocado and grape commercialization)
3. **Dehydrated Products** (Chavin Dried - separate brand, expansion phase)

## Financial Data
- **2024 Revenue:** ~US$20-22 million
- **2025 Projection:** ~US$35 million (59% growth projected)
- **Employees:** 500+
- **Gender Equity:** 51% female, 49% male
- **Small Farmers Benefited:** 500+

## Production Assets
- **Own Land:** 580 hectares in Casma, Ancash
  - 250 hectares in production
- **Partner Farmers:** 1,200 hectares (frozen products: strawberry, mango, avocado)

## Production Capacity & Infrastructure

### Frozen Products Facility
- **Production Capacity:** 7,000 kg/hr
- **Storage Capacity:** 70 FCLs
- **Technology:** IQF (Individual Quick Freezing) technology
- **2024 Volume:** ~470 containers
- **2025 Target:** ~600 containers
- **Future Target:** 800-1,000 containers/year
- **Expansion Status:** Second freezing plant under construction (investment US$4+ million), operational April (year not specified in sources but recent articles suggest 2024-2025 timeframe)
- **Claim:** Will be Peru's largest frozen plant after expansion (134% capacity increase)

### Dried Products Facility
- **Production Capacity:** 2,000 kg/day

## Quality & Traceability
- Complete traceability system
- Certified fields with controlled holistic cultivation
- Continuous staff training at field and packing levels
- Regular third-party audits
- Cultivation advisors oversee from seed selection to harvest

## Competitive Position
- Acknowledged commodity market pressure in frozen mango
- Originally one of few frozen exporters, now increased competition
- Investing in efficiency and automation to maintain competitiveness
- Focuses on working with first-tier exporters (e.g., selling fresh mango to Sunshine)
- Products not qualifying as fresh go to value-added frozen line
